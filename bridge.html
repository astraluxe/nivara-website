Three clicks, and no terminal

adris is built for people who run a business, not a build server. You never open a command line, and you never paste an API key.

  1. 01

    Pick your AI at the top of the window

    One menu in the title bar decides what the whole app runs on — the chat, the agents, the automations, every module. Choose Your Claude Code or Your Codex.

  2. 02

    adris installs it for you

    If the tool is not on your machine, adris fetches and sets it up in its own folder — nothing is installed system-wide and nothing else on your computer is touched.

  3. 03

    Sign in once, with the account you already have

    You sign in to Anthropic or OpenAI directly. adris never sees your password, and the login is the normal one you would use anywhere else.

What we will not pretend

A page like this is usually where the caveats get buried. Here they are instead.

Your subscription is billed as a subscription — we check

There is a real trap here, and it has cost people money: running these tools the wrong way bills them as API usage rather than against the subscription you already pay for. adris checks which one you are on and refuses to run if connecting would quietly put your work on a metered API bill. If we cannot confirm it is your subscription, we stop and tell you, rather than spending your money to find out.

Your limits are yours, and you can see them

A subscription has usage windows. adris reads them from the tool's own records on your machine and shows you where you are in the 5-hour and weekly window, so running out is something you see coming instead of something that happens mid-task.

What leaves your computer, exactly

When you use the bridge, what you ask goes to Anthropic or OpenAI under your own account and their terms — the same as if you had typed it into their app yourself. It does not come to us. adris.tech stores none of your business data, on this path or any other. If you would rather nothing left the machine at all, run a local model instead and the app works offline.

You can disconnect

One button, in the same menu. It signs the tool out and adris goes back to whatever you choose next. Nothing is held hostage.

Or don't use it at all

The bridge is one of four ways to power adris. The menu is the same for all of them, and you can change your mind at any time.
