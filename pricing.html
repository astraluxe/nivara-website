Questions

The short answers

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Yes. adris used to sell hosted AI, which cost us money per message. Now it runs on the AI you connect, so a user costs us nothing — and we charge nothing. We make money building custom adris setups and agentic AI offices for businesses.

Do I need to pay for an AI provider?

No. NVIDIA and Groq give out free keys, and local models are free too. If you already pay for Claude or ChatGPT, adris can use that subscription instead.

Are there any limits?

None from us. The provider you connect may have its own limits (free keys allow a certain number of requests per minute) — if you hit one, switch to another AI from the menu.

I paid for a plan before. What happens now?

Every feature is now unlocked for every account, so you keep everything. No further charges will be made. If you have any billing question, contact us.

What does a custom build involve?

Tell us what your business does and what you would like adris to handle. We will reply within 24–48 hours to scope it with you. Request a custom build.
