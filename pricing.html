Questions people actually ask

What is a task?

Roughly one meaningful AI request — a caption, a short research answer, a code review. About 1,000 tokens. Simple requests use less; deep analysis of a long document uses more. The app shows both, tasks first.

What happens if we go over?

You are never blocked mid-task. Turn on auto top-up, or buy a pack when you need one. Admins can set a hard cap so spending never exceeds an approved budget.

Can we use our own Gemini, OpenAI or Claude key?

Yes, on every plan including Free. Usage on your own key never counts against your allowance. You can also run models entirely on your own hardware, for nothing.

Do local automations use our quota?

No. Anything that runs entirely on your machine costs you nothing.

Where does our data go?

Conversations, credentials and your knowledge graph stay on your own machines. Cloud sync is off by default and covers only what you switch on.

    Common questions

    Platform AI

    Which AI model do I get on each plan?

    Every plan — free and paid — runs on the same latest, most capable AI model, included with no API key needed. Plans do not differ by model quality; they differ only by how many tokens you get per month. Higher plans simply give you a larger monthly token budget.

    What is a token, and how does it relate to tasks?

    A token is the unit AI models use to read and write text — roughly ¾ of a word (so ~1,000 tokens ≈ 750 words). One typical “task” (a caption, a short research answer, a code review) uses around 1,000 tokens. That’s why, for example, Solo’s 4M tokens works out to roughly 4,000 tasks a month. Non-technical users can think in tasks; technical users can budget by tokens.

    Can I use my own API key on a paid plan?

    Yes — on every plan. Connect your Gemini, OpenAI or Claude key and use it alongside or instead of the included AI; that usage never counts toward your quota. You can also run a model entirely on your own machine, which is free and works offline.

    Can I control which of those each part of the app uses?

    Yes. Settings › Where AI runs lets you pick the included AI, a specific one of your own keys, or a specific local model — and that choice applies to background work like Guard scans and automations. The Krew chat has its own switch in the connection bar, so you can run a quick chat locally while a long research task uses the hosted AI. Running out of your monthly allowance never blocks your own key or a local model.

    What counts as an AI task?

    Roughly one meaningful AI request — writing a caption, running research, doing a code review. Simple short requests use fewer resources; deep analysis of large documents uses more. Think of it as a guide, not a hard counter.

    Automation

    What counts as an automation run?

    Each time a saved automation fires — triggered by a schedule, an email, or a file drop. Creating, editing or pausing automations uses no runs.

    Do local automations count toward my quota?

    No. Automations that run entirely on your machine (local triggers, local actions, no cloud functions) don’t consume cloud run credits.

    Mesh & devices

    What is Mesh?

    Mesh connects your devices (PC, laptop, phone) so they can share AI tasks and files over an encrypted tunnel — no cloud storage needed. Each plan includes a device limit for your Mesh network.

    Payments & billing

    Can I pay in Indian Rupees?

    Yes. Every paid plan shows both ₹ and $ prices with separate payment buttons. Indian payments go through Razorpay in INR; international payments are handled via email within 2 hours.

    Is there a free plan?

    Yes. Download adris.tech and get the full platform — all modules included. Just bring your own API key or run local models. No credit card, no trial expiry.
