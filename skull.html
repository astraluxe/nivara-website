How we work

Trust is not a promise. It is a process.

  1. 01

    Written scope first

    Every check names what it covers, agreed before it starts. No scope, no scan, and that rule is in our software, not just on this page.

  2. 02

    Read only by default

    We look, we do not change. Anything that would change your systems is your decision, in writing.

  3. 03

    No hacking back

    No access to systems that are not yours or your client's, ever. Who might be behind an incident stays a confidential hypothesis.

  4. 04

    Evidence over promises

    No guarantees. A report says what was checked, when, and what it could not check.

  5. 05

    Our own office first

    A weekly read only audit of our own machines and code, and an append only log of everything skull does.

Free security check

Your security deserves a second pair of eyes.

skull is new and trust has to be earned, so the first check is free. A read only review of your website's public setup and, if you give access, one code repository scanned for leaked keys and vulnerable parts. You get the full report and owe us nothing.

Ask for a free check →
  1. 01RequestTell us what to check. We agree the scope in writing.
  2. 02ReviewA read only check, inside that scope and nothing else.
  3. 03ReceiveA report with every finding, its fix, and what was not checked.
Do you need access to my systems?

Not for the website check: it uses only what is public. For a code check you choose the repository and give read only access, which you can remove at any time.

What happens to my data?

We keep only what the report needs, use it only for your check, and delete it when you ask. Nothing about you is published.

What exactly is in scope?

Whatever we agree in writing before we start, and nothing else. If we notice something outside the scope, we tell you, and we do not test it.

How long does it take?

We agree the timing with you when we agree the scope, based on what is being checked.

Is it really free?

The first check is. If you want more after the report, we quote it separately, and you are under no obligation.
