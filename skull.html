What we do

Find, secure, trace and prove. Legally.

Secure your systems

Leaked keys and passwords in your code, outdated parts with known holes, and a hardening checklist for your website and cloud, with the fix for each finding.

Compliance readiness

Checklists and evidence trackers for the DPDP Act, CERT-In's directions, ISO 27001 and SOC 2, so you know what is missing before an auditor or a customer asks.

Trace and prove

For victims of crypto theft: stolen funds followed across public blockchains, with an evidence pack for the police, the exchange or your lawyer. Public data only.

How we work

The rules we do not bend.

  • Written scope first. Every check names what it covers, agreed before it starts. No scope, no scan, and that rule is in our software, not just on this page.
  • No hacking back. No access to systems that are not yours or your client's, ever.
  • No public accusations. Who might be behind an incident stays a confidential hypothesis.
  • No guarantees. A report says what was checked, when, and what it could not check.
  • Our own office first. A weekly read-only audit of our own machines and code, and an append-only log of everything skull does.

Our first checks are free.

skull is new and trust has to be earned. A read-only review of your website's public setup and, if you give access, one code repository scanned for leaked keys and vulnerable parts. You get the full report and owe us nothing.
