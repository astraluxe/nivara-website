Sales partner programme · open to firms across India

Your clients need an office that runs itself. Earn when they get one.

adris.tech builds AI departments for small and medium businesses: tender reading, enquiries, follow-ups and quotations, with the owner approving what goes out. If you already advise these businesses as a Tally partner, CA, tender consultant, IT dealer or ERP implementer, introduce us and earn a share of what each client pays, under a simple written agreement.

15%

Referral partner

You introduce a business; we sell, set up and support. 15% of the client's first-year software, plus 5% of their second year.

25%

Reseller

You present and sell, and stay the client's first contact. 25% of the first year, then 10% of every renewal while you look after the client.

20%

IT integrator

You sell and set up the computers. 20% of the first year, 7.5% of renewals, and 3% of the hardware when we build the machine.

How it works

  1. Apply with the form. We reply within one working day and fix a 15-minute call.
  2. Sign a short partner agreement. Nothing starts before both sides sign.
  3. Register a client with us by email before you introduce them. They are yours for 6 months.
  4. Get paid within 15 days of each payment the client makes, with a monthly statement.

The fine print, in plain words

  • Commission is on the software amount the client has paid, before GST.
  • From your 3rd client in a financial year the first-year rate rises by 3 points, and from the 6th by 5 points (never above 30%).
  • If a client is refunded within 90 days, that commission is returned.
  • TDS is deducted where the law requires; add GST to your invoice if you are registered.
  • Not exclusive: work with whoever else you like.

Example: a client paying Rs 1,00,000 for the first year earns a reseller Rs 25,000.
